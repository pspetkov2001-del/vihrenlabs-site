SAP ECC mainstream maintenance ends December 31, 2027.

Most IT teams have done the math. Roughly.

What's often missing from that calculation:

**The real cut-off isn't December 2027. It's the summer of 2025.**

Not because SAP moves up the date. Because of lead time.

A typical mid-market S/4HANA migration — brownfield conversion, 2,000-5,000 users, 1 primary instance — takes 18-24 months from project kick-off to go-live. Add 3-6 months for vendor selection, business case approval, and resource allocation.

You need to start now if you haven't already.

The organizations that started in 2023 are live or in hypercare. The ones that started in 2024 are mid-project. The ones starting in 2025 are going to find that the best system integrators are booked through Q3 2026 — because everyone else also ran the math late.

**The second error: treating go-live as the finish line.**

It isn't. Go-live is the start of hypercare, which typically runs 3-6 months. The most dangerous period isn't the cutover weekend — it's month 2 of hypercare, when the consultants are winding down and the internal team is running the system for the first time without a safety net.

Organizations that don't build hypercare staffing into the original business case either extend consultant contracts at emergency rates or accept a degraded post-go-live experience that erodes user adoption.

**What "on track" actually means in an S/4 project**

Four indicators that matter more than Gantt chart green:

1. Business partner conversion error count (should be trending to zero by T-8 weeks)
2. Master data freeze compliance rate (exceptions should be logged and governance-approved, not verbally waived)
3. Cutover run-sheet owner-per-step coverage (every step has a named human who rehearsed it)
4. Rollback criteria documented and agreed by the steering committee before T-day

Most project status reports don't track these. They track milestone completion and RAG status, which are both lagging indicators.

If you're heading into an S/4 migration, these are the numbers worth watching.
